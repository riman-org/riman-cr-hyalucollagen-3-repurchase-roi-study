--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Overview

Background: The introduction of patented bioactive complexes into tropical markets requires rigorous validation of sensory performance against local humidity profiles. Methods: A stratified survey (N=200) was conducted across three distinct Costa Rican microclimates (Limon, San Jose, Liberia) to assess the Hyalucollagen-3 complex. Quantitative Results: Overall composite mean was 3.43 with a Top-2-Box score of 45.6%. Notably, 94.0% of respondents affirmed the immediate cooling/soothing benefit for post-sun exposure (Q03), while only 0.5% trusted the '1000x water attraction' claim without clinical backing (Q04). NPS stood at -28.0, driven by distribution barriers (87.5%) and long-term efficacy uncertainty (98.5%). Strategic Implications: Conversion from starter kits to full rituals is heavily dependent on visible results within 2 weeks (100% selection rate). Affluent segments show higher tolerance for premium pricing but demand strict regulatory compliance evidence. This study underscores the necessity of localized clinical data to bridge the trust gap in emerging Latin American markets.
